Privacy Policy — Slope Puzzle

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Slope Puzzle (package name com.ulhasch.slopepuzzle), referred to below as "the app".

Short version

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network requests. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any device identifier. It requests no Android permissions beyond what is needed to run.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device's standard app storage:

  • Your best score and the number of games played
  • Your sound on/off preference
  • Your vibration on/off preference
  • Your display mode preference
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial screen

This information never leaves your device and is not readable by us or by anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social media integrations. No data is shared with any third party.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that handle data — for example advertising or online leaderboards — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top of this page will change.
